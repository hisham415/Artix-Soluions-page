Biography Overview.

Mohsen Hesham is a Full Stack Developer from Egypt and a member of Artix Solutions. His work combines Laravel backend development with responsive React interfaces, with an interest in clean architecture, RESTful APIs and database design.

His public technical profile lists a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence from MTI University, alongside React and PHP/Laravel internships at the Information Technology Institute (ITI). His projects span recipe discovery, job listings and a surface defect detection graduation project.

Mohsen brings a practical focus on connecting interfaces, application logic and data. His interests also include competitive programming, data structures and algorithms, and UML modeling.

My skills

  • Backend: PHP, Laravel, RESTful APIs and MVC architecture
  • Frontend: React, JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3 and Tailwind CSS
  • Data: MySQL, database design and query optimization
  • Tools: Git, GitHub, Postman and UML diagrams

Work & speciality fields

Backend services, responsive web applications and relational data modeling are central to Mohsen's work. His surface defect detection project connects Laravel and MySQL with an AI-assisted inspection workflow, while Recipes explores a React-based user experience.

Education & experience

  • B.Sc. in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence — MTI University
  • PHP & Laravel Developer Internship — ITI, August–September 2024
  • React Developer Internship — ITI, August–September 2023
